US-RF02-2 
Lockout by attempts and closure by inactivity 
User Story: As a Security Administrator, I want to lock accounts after 5 failed attempts 
for 15 minutes and close the session after 30 minutes of inactivity to reduce risks. So that 
I can ensure security by preventing unauthorized access and closing sessions after 
prolonged inactivity. 
Acceptance Criteria: Given 5 failed attempts, when the sixth occurs within the window, 
then the account is locked for 15 min. Given a user inactive for 30 min, when they try to 
navigate, then they see the login screen.
