Alerts and report of expired data under review 
User Story: As a DPO, I want alerts and reports when expired records marked for 
extended retention require legal review, so that I can decide whether to retain, suppress, 
or anonymize them. 
Acceptance Criteria: Given a record under review, when its period expires, then the 
system feeds a visible report requesting a decision.
