Name 
Manage Training 
Created by 
Team 6 DataLegal 
Description 
Comprehensive module for DPOs to create, assign, track, and manage 
data protection training programs, including course materials, 
completion tracking, and certification issuance. 
Actors 
DPO 
Extends 
None 
Preconditions 
1. The DPO must be authenticated with training management 
permissions. 
2. Training materials and courses must be uploaded to the system. 
3. Employee user accounts must be active and assigned to 
departments. 
 
 
 

 
Postconditions 
1. Training courses are assigned to target employees or departments. 
2. Employee progress is tracked (Not Started/In Progress/Completed). 
3. Training completion data is available for compliance reporting. 
4. Certificates are issued upon successful course completion. 
Flow 
1. DPO accesses the "Training Management" module and selects 
"Create New Training Plan". 
2. DPO defines training parameters including target audience, course 
selection, completion deadline, and mandatory/optional designation. 
3. DPO activates the training plan and the system sends email 
notifications to assigned employees. 
4. DPO monitors training progress through the dashboard showing 
completion percentages and overdue assignments. 
5. When an employee completes a course, the system automatically 
issues a digital certificate. 
6. DPO generates training compliance reports for audit or regulatory 
purposes.

Analyze Processed Data Panel

Team 6 DataLegal 
 
 
 

 
Description 
Provides executives with interactive analytical panels to visualize and 
analyze the organization's data processing landscape, identifying 
trends, compliance gaps, and risk concentrations across departments 
and data categories. 
Actors 
Executive 
Extends 
None 
Preconditions 
1. The Executive must be authenticated with viewing permissions. 
2. Data processing activities must be registered and classified in the 
system. 
3. Risk assessments must be completed for meaningful analysis. 
4. The system must meet performance requirements. 
Postconditions 
1. Executive gains strategic insights into organizational data 
processing activities. 
2. Compliance gaps and high-risk areas are identified for management 
attention. 
3. Analysis results can be exported for board presentations or strategic 
planning. 
4. Viewed analysis is logged for audit purposes. 
Flow 
1. Executive accesses the "Data Processing Analysis Panel" from the 
executive dashboard. 
2. The system displays multiple analytical views including heatmaps, 
distribution charts, and trend lines for data processing activities. 
 
 
 

 
3. Executive selects specific filters to refine the analysis (date range, 
department, risk level, data category). 
4. The system dynamically updates all visualizations based on selected 
filters. 
5. Executive clicks on an identified area in the visualization for 
drill-down to view detailed records. 
6. Executive selects "Export Analysis Report" and the system 
generates a comprehensive PDF with key findings and visualizations. 
7. Executive downloads the report and the system logs the analysis 
session in the audit trail.
