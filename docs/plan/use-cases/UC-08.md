UC008 
Name 
Manage incidents and breaches 
Created by 
Team 6 DataLegal 
Description 
Records security incidents and manages the mandatory notifications to 
the SPDP and data subjects within legal deadlines. 
Actors 
DPO, Security Manager 
Extends 
None 
Preconditions 
1. A security incident has been detected with a confirmed date and 
discovery time. 
2. Affected data categories and the volume of data subjects have been 
identified. 
 
 
 

 
Postconditions 
1. Incident is classified by impact and recorded in the immutable audit 
log. 
2. Notification status is tracked via a traffic-light scheme to ensure 
deadline compliance. 
Flow 
1. Open the "Incidents and Breaches" module. 
2. Register incident details including type, discovery date, and volume 
of affected subjects. 
3. Select the impact level (Low, Medium, High, or Critical). 
4. Trigger the automatic generation of notification drafts for the SPDP 
and Data Subjects. 
5. Review the notification content and verify the delivery to the 
intended recipients. 
6. Monitor the status traffic-light to ensure the 5-day (SPDP) and 
3-day (Subject) legal deadlines are met. 
7. Close the incident record once all legal actions are completed.

Name 
Create an action plan 
Created by 
Team 6 DataLegal 
Description 
Allows the definition of remediation tasks, responsible parties, and 
target dates to mitigate identified risks or audit findings. 
 
 
 

 
Actors 
DPO, Department Head 
Extends 
None 
Preconditions 
1. A risk assessment result or audit finding must exist. 
2. Responsible users must be registered in the system. 
Postconditions 
1. The plan is active and tasks are assigned to responsible parties. 
2. Risk scores are updated upon task completion. 
Flow 
1. Select a high-risk activity or audit finding to remediate. 
2. Input the plan title, priority level, and general description. 
3. Add individual tasks defining the specific mitigation action. 
4. Assign a responsible user and a target completion date for each task. 
5. Confirm and activate the plan to trigger email notifications to 
assignees. 
6. Monitor task progress (Pending, In Progress, Completed) via the 
dashboard.
