UC010 
Name 
Assign Role 
 
 
 

 
Created by 
Team 6 DataLegal 
Description 
Allows the Administrator to assign or modify user roles within the 
system, defining their access level and permissions across different 
modules according to their organizational responsibilities. 
Actors 
Administrator 
Extends 
None 
Preconditions 
1. The Administrator must be authenticated with full administrative 
privileges. 
2. The user account must exist in the system. 
3. Organizational roles must be defined (DPO, Auditor, Department 
Head, Executive). 
Postconditions 
1. User is assigned a new role with corresponding permissions. 
2. Previous role permissions are revoked if role is changed. 
3. User receives notification about their new role and access rights. 
4. Role assignment is recorded in the immutable audit log. 
Flow 
1. Administrator accesses the "User Management" module and selects 
a specific user. 
2. The system displays the user's current profile including their 
assigned role. 
3. Administrator clicks "Change Role" and selects the new role from 
available options (DPO, Auditor, Department Head, Executive, 
Administrator). 
 
 
 

 
4. The system displays a summary of permissions associated with the 
selected role. 
5. Administrator confirms the role assignment. 
6. The system updates the user's permissions matrix and sends an 
automated email notification. 
7. The system records the role change in the audit log with timestamp 
and administrator identity.

Description 
Comprehensive module for DPOs to create, assign, track, and manage 
data protection training programs, including course materials, 
completion tracking, and certification issuance. 
Actors 
DPO 
Extends 
None 
Preconditions 
1. The DPO must be authenticated with training management 
permissions. 
2. Training materials and courses must be uploaded to the system. 
3. Employee user accounts must be active and assigned to 
departments. 
 
 
 

 
Postconditions 
1. Training courses are assigned to target employees or departments. 
2. Employee progress is tracked (Not Started/In Progress/Completed). 
3. Training completion data is available for compliance reporting. 
4. Certificates are issued upon successful course completion. 
Flow 
1. DPO accesses the "Training Management" module and selects 
"Create New Training Plan". 
2. DPO defines training parameters including target audience, course 
selection, completion deadline, and mandatory/optional designation. 
3. DPO activates the training plan and the system sends email 
notifications to assigned employees. 
4. DPO monitors training progress through the dashboard showing 
completion percentages and overdue assignments. 
5. When an employee completes a course, the system automatically 
issues a digital certificate. 
6. DPO generates training compliance reports for audit or regulatory 
purposes.
