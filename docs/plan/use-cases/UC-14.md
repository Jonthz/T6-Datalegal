```
track 
comprehensive data protection audits with structured workflows for 
evidence collection, findings management, and corrective action 
follow-up. 
Actors 
Auditor 
Extends 
None 
Preconditions 
1. The Auditor must be authenticated with audit management 
permissions. 
2. Audit scope (departments, processes, systems, or treatment 
activities) must be defined. 
3. Audit checklists and compliance standards must be available in the 
system. 
Postconditions 
1. Audit plan is documented with clear objectives, scope, and timeline. 
 
 
 

 
2. Audit findings are recorded, classified by severity, and assigned for 
remediation. 
3. Comprehensive audit report is generated and stored in the 
immutable repository. 
4. Corrective action plans are created and tracked to closure. 
Flow 
1. Auditor accesses the "Audit Management" module and selects "Plan 
New Audit". 
2. Auditor defines audit parameters (title, type, scope, dates, team 
members, checklist template) and saves the plan. 
3. The system sends notifications to auditees and on the start date, 
Auditor initiates the audit. 
4. Auditor assigns significant findings to responsible parties for 
remediation with target dates. 
5. After completing audit areas, Auditor initiates report generation and 
the system compiles findings into a structured report. 
6. Auditor reviews, finalizes, and submits the report for DPO 
approval. 
7. Auditor monitors corrective action progress, conducts follow-up 
verification, and closes the audit.

Manage Data Protection and Compliance 
 
 
 

 
Created by 
Team 6 DataLegal 
Description 
Central module for DPOs to oversee and coordinate all data protection 
compliance activities, including monitoring compliance status, 
managing regulatory obligations, and ensuring ongoing adherence to 
LOPDP requirements. 
Actors 
DPO 
Extends 
None 
Preconditions 
1. The DPO must be authenticated with full compliance management 
permissions. 
2. Organizational profile and treatment activities must be registered. 
3. Compliance framework and regulatory requirements must be 
configured in the system. 
Postconditions 
1. Overall compliance status is monitored and up-to-date. 
2. Compliance gaps are identified and flagged for action. 
3. Regulatory obligations are tracked with deadline alerts. 
4. Compliance evidence is organized and readily available for 
regulatory inspections. 
Flow 
1. DPO accesses the "Data Protection and Compliance" central 
module. 
2. The system displays the compliance dashboard with overall score 
and traffic light indicators for different compliance areas. 
3. DPO reviews areas showing yellow or red status and clicks on a 
specific compliance area for drill-down details. 
 
 
 

 
4. DPO identifies compliance gaps and uses integrated functions to 
address them (assign training, create action plans, send reminders). 
5. DPO accesses the "Regulatory Obligations Tracker" to view 
upcoming deadlines and marks obligations as completed with 
evidence. 
6. DPO organizes documentation in the "Compliance Evidence 
Repository" for regulatory readiness. 
7. DPO generates comprehensive compliance status reports for 
executive management or regulatory authorities. 
8. DPO sets up automated alerts for upcoming deadlines, compliance 
score drops, or new high-risk activities.
```
