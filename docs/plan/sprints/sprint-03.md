Sprint 3 Backlog: Compliance Functionalities and Advanced Management 
This sprint is crucial for materializing the core value of the DataLegal tool by developing the 
workflows that support active regulatory compliance. This includes: 
●​ Implementing the Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA). 
●​ Centralized management of ARCO requests (Access, Rectification, Cancellation, and 
Opposition). 
●​ Generation of the Records of Processing Activities (ROPA). 
●​ Automation of risk-based action plans. 
Once the central compliance workflows have materialized, the following sprint will be dedicated to 
refining the platform with advanced auditing and documentation capabilities.
